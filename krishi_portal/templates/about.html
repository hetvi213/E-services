About Krishi E-Government Services

A citizen-friendly digital platform making essential government services clearer, faster, and more accessible.

Our story

One portal. Trusted assistance.

Krishi E-Government Services is an initiative focused on making government documentation easier, faster, and more accessible for everyone.

Whether citizens need to apply for a PAN card, update Aadhaar details, or request an official certificate, our platform brings service information, document guidance, secure enquiries, and application support together in a few simple steps.

Our Vision

To create a unified, citizen-friendly digital platform that simplifies access to government services while ensuring transparency, efficiency, security, and trust for every individual.

Our Mission

To simplify applications for government documents, support transparent communication, enable secure interactions, and guide citizens from their first enquiry through completion.
